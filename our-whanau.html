Our Whānau

Whānau-owned. Community-focused. Built on meaningful mahi.

Results Contracting Services is a whānau-owned Northland business built on hard work, community connection, and a commitment to creating meaningful employment.

Our Story

Our story

Rewiri and his wife Jess relocated home to Northland in October 2020 to make a difference in the Northland community.

Results Contracting Services was established to work hand in hand with local communities, businesses, and individuals to provide employment, mentorship, support, and development pathways.

Their goal was to create a robust platform that encourages the development of employment opportunities across Northland while delivering quality contracting services that local people can rely on.

Local Ownership

Whānau owned and locally operated

Results Contracting Services is jointly owned by Rewiri and Jess.

Rewiri works full-time in the business and manages all projects, ensuring every job is completed with care, communication, and attention to detail.

As a growing small business, RCS is proud to support local people, local projects, and local outcomes.

Team Development

Growing people as well as projects

We nurture our team through continuous improvement and learning.

Our team members are encouraged to attend courses and workshops that support upskilling, confidence, and long-term development. Each team member has a personal development plan that outlines learning objectives, goals, and targets.

This approach helps ensure our team is prepared, capable, and ready to deliver the best possible service to our customers.

Meaningful Employment

Creating meaningful employment

Our workforce strategy is about creating meaningful employment.

We do this by providing mentoring, coaching, and individual support for our team members. We also maintain strong health and safety practices to care for our team's holistic wellbeing while at work.

For us, good business is about more than completing jobs. It is about creating opportunities, supporting people, and contributing positively to our communities.
